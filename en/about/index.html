About the studio

Dino Studio.

Visual production with an editorial eye.

Dino — photographer, filmmaker and photojournalist in Stockholm.

Dino Studio works across photography, film and photojournalism from Stockholm.

I have worked for many years across photography and moving image, with a focus on people, sport, performances and events.

My education and experience in studio and multi-camera production, together with training in IT support and networking, have given me a strong technical foundation for planning and working on productions.

My studies in project management have also strengthened the way I plan, coordinate and manage projects from the first idea through production and final delivery.

This combination of visual experience, technical knowledge and project management allows me to work across both the creative and practical sides of a production, while solving problems quickly and keeping the process clear and structured.

Dino Studio works directly with clients from the initial brief and planning to photography, filming, editing and final delivery.
