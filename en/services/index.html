Event Content System — Photo reportage and short highlight film from your event, delivered organised and ready to use.

Built around your event so your organisation gets a complete photo reportage, a short highlight film, or both. Editing and colour correction are delivered by the studio, with files organised for press, social channels, partner communication, and internal archives.

—Event photo reportage covering speakers, stage moments, awards, guests, and atmosphere
—Short highlight film capturing the key moments and energy of the event
—Editing and colour correction delivered by the studio in original aspect ratios

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about usage rights, delivery times, travel, and bookings.

01What usage rights are included?

Usage rights are tailored to your needs and stated clearly in the written quote — typically covering your organisation’s own communication channels (website, social media, newsletters, press releases, and internal use), with extended commercial or third-party licensing agreed when needed.

02How is the delivery time agreed?

The delivery timeline depends on the scope of the assignment and is agreed in your written quote before production. If your team needs a small selection of press or social media images shortly after an event, let us know in your enquiry so we can plan for it.

03Do you take assignments outside Stockholm?

Yes. Dino Studio is based in Stockholm and is available for assignments across Sweden and internationally. For projects outside Greater Stockholm, travel and any required accommodation are specified in the written quote.

04How do editing and review rounds work?

Editing and colour correction are delivered by the studio. For highlight films, motion projects, and digital design, the review process and included feedback rounds are agreed in the written quote.

05What is included in a standard delivery?

Preparation and schedule review prior to the shoot, on-site photography and/or filming, selection, editing and colour correction delivered by the studio, and organised digital delivery in high-resolution and web-ready formats as agreed in the quote.

06Can we enquire about shorter sessions, portraits, or product shoots?

Yes. In addition to full-day events, you can enquire about shorter assignments in Stockholm such as press briefings, award presentations, studio or workplace portraits, product photography, or digital design.
